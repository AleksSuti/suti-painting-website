Services

Painting & finishing, done properly

We specialise in wall painting, ceiling painting, drywall repair, surface preparation and high-quality finishes — for homes and businesses across Belgium, the Netherlands and Luxembourg.

01

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors and built-ins. Clean cutting-in, even coverage, and colour that goes on the way it looked on the swatch.

02

Exterior Painting

Facades, woodwork, window frames and masonry, finished with weather-resistant coatings suited to the Belgian, Dutch and Luxembourg climate.

03

Wall & Ceiling Painting

Full rooms or single accent walls, including textured and multi-toned finishes where you want something beyond flat colour.

04

Drywall Repair

Cracks, holes, water stains and uneven joints repaired and smoothed before any paint goes near the wall.

05

Surface Preparation

Sanding, patching, priming and masking — the unglamorous work that makes a paint job actually last.

06

Residential & Commercial

Single rooms, full homes, offices and commercial spaces. We scope and schedule around what the property needs.

07

Industrial Protective Coating

Corrosion-resistant coating for structural steel, beams and canopy framing on large-scale construction and industrial builds.

08

Multi-Storey & Facility Exteriors

Full-building repaints on apartment complexes and commercial facilities, coordinated around occupied units and site access.
