About Us

Painting is detail work. We treat it that way.

Suti Painting & Finishes provides professional interior and exterior painting services for residential and commercial properties across Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg and Germany, including industrial protective coating for structural steel on large-scale builds. We specialise in wall painting, ceiling painting, drywall repair, surface preparation, and high-quality finishes, with a focus on reliable service, attention to detail, and outstanding results on every project.

Our experience was built over more than ten years across the United States and Europe, including specialised training in industrial coating and large-scale painting projects. Today, we operate primarily in Belgium, bringing that same level of skill and standards to every residential, commercial and industrial project we take on.
